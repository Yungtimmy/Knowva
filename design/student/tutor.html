Why do plants need light to make food?

Short answer

Light gives the plant the energy to turn water and carbon dioxide into glucose.

Step by step

  1. Chlorophyll in the leaf traps light.
  2. That energy splits water, releasing oxygen.
  3. The plant uses the stored energy to build glucose from carbon dioxide.

Remember it

Light in, sugar made, oxygen out.
