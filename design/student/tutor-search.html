Why do plants need light to make food?

Light gives the plant energy to turn water and carbon dioxide into glucose. Chlorophyll in the leaf traps that light.

Today

What happens in the light reactions?

Short answer

Water is split and the energy is stored for the next stage.

Step by step

  1. Light hits chlorophyll in the thylakoid.
  2. That energy splits water, releasing oxygen.
  3. The energy is stored as ATP and NADPH.

Remember it

Light in, water split, oxygen out.

So the oxygen we breathe comes from water?

Yes. The oxygen released comes from the split water, not from carbon dioxide.
